OUR COMPANY

About UP Gas

UP Gas is an LPG gas supplier serving homes, families, restaurants, businesses and other customers in Pienaar, Mbombela and surrounding communities. We make it easier for customers to access dependable cooking and heating gas through convenient refills, cylinder sales and delivery where available.

Our Mission

To provide safe, reliable and affordable LPG gas that helps households and businesses cook, heat and operate comfortably every day.

What We Do

We supply LPG cylinder refills and cylinders in different sizes, with selected refill services available in-store and delivery available for qualifying orders.

Safety First

We believe LPG must be handled responsibly. We aim to provide dependable products and service while keeping safe gas handling at the centre of every customer interaction.

Our Customers

From everyday home cooking to restaurants and business operations, UP Gas is focused on making LPG access simple, convenient and dependable.
